# Graduated advisory restrictions in safety-critical decision support
## PhD novelty and journal assessment · 16 November 2026

## 1. Executive assessment

**Revise.** A PhD-level paper is plausible if it estimates when output-specific restrictions improve independently judged advisory safety at comparable useful coverage, and identifies when that advantage disappears. Graduated restriction, runtime shielding, and the safety–performance trade-off have substantial precedents. CAP is a particularly close method precedent that already balances specific predictions, set-valued predictions and abstention using downstream utility. [CAP paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v304/tayebati26a.html) An unrestricted/binary/graduated comparison by itself is weak novelty. The strongest unit of contribution is an empirically tested boundary for when useful lower-risk advice survives a loss of observability.

The conference replay's 5.81% of 9,135 eligible hours describes a change in the permitted set. The alternative data configuration reports 4.48%, with both source and period differing. Neither quantity estimates harm, frequency of changed displayed recommendations, or independent sample size. The existing Journal 1 evaluation specification also reports that the tested Layer 3 configuration emitted only Delay and had no active SAFE rules. Its fidelity results establish implementation behaviour for that configuration. A credible outcome study needs a candidate pool capable of producing both useful and independently inappropriate advice across the key cases. The local drafts were consulted as project records. Publication status was left unverified. ## 3. Closest competing research

1. **Jansen et al.** Already parameterizes action availability using relative probabilistic risk and reports performance consequences with and without a shield. A threshold sweep of our own gate repeats the logic. The remaining question is whether independently unacceptable human-facing advice is preferentially removed at comparable useful coverage. [CONCUR paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CONCUR.2020.3)
2. **Risk-aware shielding of POMCP policies.** Trace-derived rules filter options in belief space. This anticipates rule-based selection, noisy observation and performance-focused evaluation. Independent appraisal of displayed advice and explicitly matched participation are distinct from the planning evaluation. [Artificial Intelligence paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2023.103987)
3. **Carr et al.** Models partial observability directly and tests shielding plus later removal. Its positive and conservatism findings undermine claims that evaluating restriction under incomplete information is new. Forecast age, correlated observation loss and advisory specificity offer a different applied test. [AAAI paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/26723)
4. **Gerold and Lucia.** Couples uncertain inputs, adaptive conservatism, fewer interventions and safety. An uncertainty × gating interaction alone provides little originality. A human-facing reference and measured availability of actionable alternatives define a different endpoint. [Computers & Chemical Engineering paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compchemeng.2025.109521)
5. **Paterno et al.** Operational severity tiers and differential restrictions are already studied in human-facing DSS. Compliance is the key endpoint and site differences weaken causal interpretation. Holding a shared candidate pool, outer cutoff and decision context fixed improves identification of the intermediate filter effect. [JAMIA paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2605599/)
6. **Strom et al.** Randomization shows that strong intervention can improve a targeted process measure while creating other clinically material harm. A fisheries experiment must count omitted warning, verification or safe departure opportunities, depending on task and evidence. [Trial record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20876410/)
7. **Maritime adaptive human oversight.** A 2026 maritime implementation proposal already blends risk scoring, hard conditions, variable oversight, fallback and workload costs. The remaining question concerns human-facing advice, a paired component contrast and an independently held reference. [Applied Sciences paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/14/6903)
8. **Maritime fault-tolerant decision support.** Multisensor uncertainty and variable authority have been studied with recorded maritime runs. A mere transfer of graduated governance to coastal operations has limited novelty. A small-vessel departure task with actual forecast-availability data and specific advice-type labels could support different conclusions. [Sensors paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13611254/)
9. **Healthcare abstention framework.** Explicit refusal and useful mitigated engagement are already distinguished and assessed in pilots. A claim to introduce partial help under uncertainty fails. Strong differentiation would require a common candidate pool and independent, calibrated comparisons against well-tuned whole-message abstention under observation degradation. [npj Digital Medicine paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02882-1)

10. **CAP (Tayebati et al.).** Already offers an instance-level choice between a specific answer, a set of answers and full abstention, with explicit utility. A paper that just contrasts partial output with whole-message refusal therefore has limited methodological novelty. Distinguishing evidence would require independently judged physical-operational harms and omissions, realistic stale or missing inputs, and an isolated effect of category restrictions under the shared cutoff. [ACML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v304/tayebati26a.html)

**Do equivalents exist?** The core evaluation components exist. CAP already evaluates a closely analogous three-option output policy using predictive tasks and downstream utility. The reviewed clinical work compares tiered and non-tiered alerting and other studies vary mandatory versus discretionary intervention. The shielding literature compares unfiltered and risk-tuned restricted choices, with reward and violations. Across those comparisons, this review found no confirmed example of the exact three-way experiment in which the same human-facing candidate messages are rescored under independent safety criteria while holding the outer cutoff fixed. Evidence is stronger for the existence of near-equivalents than for a worldwide absence. The randomized affirmative-response comparison itself found no incremental prescribing benefit over a passive alert. It measured prescribing and changed multiple interface elements, giving a useful negative precedent with a different estimand. [Affirmative-response trial](https://doi.org/10.1136/jamia.2009.000695)

A count of intervention levels gives little differentiation because the same policies can be encoded through thresholds or output classes.

**Construct test.** A zero count of forbidden advice after deterministic filtering confirms compliance. A genuine outcome can move either way under an external rubric. Retained advice may still be unsafe, removed advice may be safe and useful, and a modified message can omit a necessary warning. Clinical results illustrate the distinction. A recent synthesis reports effects on prescribing behaviour with imprecise estimates of effects on adverse interactions. Strom's randomized trial documented treatment delays. [Clinical outcome review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12451929/), [Strom trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20876410/)

## 6. Recommended research direction

**Working title**  
*When Do Intermediate Advisory Restrictions Improve Safety at Comparable Useful Coverage? Evidence from Small-Vessel Departure Advice under Degraded Observations*

Start with predeparture decisions for a specified vessel class and route type. Distinguish a specific operational instruction (departure window or timing) from a lower-specificity action that the operator can execute (consult a named forecast or verify a material condition before deciding). Prove that the distinction matters empirically. A generic reminder has little utility if it cannot inform a next step under the scenario's access constraints.

Use A as an identification strategy, B as the practical evaluation criterion, and C as the main contribution. A compact mechanism analysis can test whether gains arise because the withheld outputs concentrate hazards and the retained outputs retain meaningful support. For a deterministic paired policy comparison with identical outputs outside the affected set, the overall risk difference equals the fraction of episodes with changed displayed advice times the mean paired risk difference in those episodes. For an episode loss bounded by one, the change rate bounds the absolute benefit. The reported replay percentage concerns admissible sets and can support that bound only in the same population if all display differences are confined to those events and the denominator matches. The algebra is a diagnostic identity. The distribution of harmful removals, lost warnings and robustness boundaries is the empirical target.

## 7. Research questions and hypotheses

Specify practical safety and useful-coverage margins with the reference panel before test data are scored. Use absolute differences in percentage points and show confidence intervals relative to those margins.

| Question | Falsifiable hypothesis | Evidence against |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1. What is the incremental effect of the intermediate restriction when the outer cutoff is shared? | H1. Graduated filtering reduces severe inappropriate output per eligible episode by at least a prespecified meaningful margin versus participation-only gating. Report the all-episode estimate and the clean CAUTION stratum defined before filtering. | Paired interval excludes the target benefit, or omissions and replacement harms offset removals. An interval spanning meaningful benefit and harm is inconclusive. |
| RQ2. Does partial advice beat tuned whole-message abstention? | H2. Over a prespecified feasible harm range, graduated filtering provides greater useful coverage at comparable severe-harm exposure or lower exposure at comparable useful coverage. Require a common range and practical margin. | A tuned binary policy matches or dominates, or curves cross without a material gain in the prespecified range. |
| RQ3. How does observation quality change the advantage? | H3. Benefit declines when a prespecified combination of forecast age and critical-channel dropout causes the gate to miss conditions required for safe specificity. The interaction occurs on held-out weather episodes and in external data. | A precise interaction estimate excludes the target decline, the proposed omission mechanism is unsupported, or the candidate boundary fails to replicate. |

## 8. Experimental design

**Comparators and isolation.**

- U: pass through the shared ranked candidate message with a neutral interface and common status notices.
- B: suppress advisories at the same outer cutoff as G, leaving the candidate message unchanged in other states.
- G: same cutoff, plus a predeclared action/category restriction in CAUTION. Hold generator inputs, candidate ranking, time budget and display format fixed. Freeze whether filtering deletes, reranks or substitutes. Explicitly test deletion-only versus substitution if replacement is central to efficacy.
- B-tuned: whole-message abstention using the same observations and candidate risk evidence, with threshold selected on calibration data. Sweep the operating threshold to estimate the frontier. A fair redesign of this comparator can use a risk score for message harm. If a human is genuinely available, benchmark deferral with measured response availability and accuracy. Treat fallback behaviour as part of the policy.
- Strict binary that suppresses CAUTION and UNSAFE brackets the price of shutting off the middle region. A calibrated action-level filter and a simple independent rule-only advisory baseline provide useful secondary challenges if implementable. Use an oracle only as a diagnostic upper bound.

**Reference layer.** For each vessel/route episode, document class, loaded condition, plausible operating limits, route and forecast horizon. Use two independent hazard views where feasible. An engineering reference can use independently sourced forecast verification or observations and a vessel/route operability assessment with inputs such as wave period, direction, steepness, loading and shelter. A blinded panel of local skippers and an independent maritime safety or naval architecture specialist should develop an action-based rubric before seeing the evaluated gate. Rate messages against the dossier without policy identifiers or gate labels, randomize order, and use a staged review to identify whether wording reveals treatment. Predefine severities and incompatible advice, misleading certainty, omitted safety-critical caveats and unjustified restriction of a feasible decision. Have overlapping raters, report agreement before adjudication and preserve disagreement or indeterminate labels for sensitivity analyses. The engineering and expert sources can share physical drivers with the gate while using separately justified thresholds, richer observations or outcomes, and no fitted test-case feedback. [Small-vessel operability study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0029801814000201), [FAO/ILO/IMO small-vessel recommendations](https://www.fao.org/4/i3108e/i3108e.pdf), [INCOIS boat-specific advisory](https://incois.gov.in/site/services/SVA_overview.jsp)

**Distinguish two references.** An ex ante reference asks whether a recommendation's specificity was supportable using information reasonably available at issue time. An ex post hazard reference asks whether it would expose the operator to concerning verified conditions if followed. Future measurements may inform the latter but must stay out of the policy inputs and the ex ante dossier. Score and report the two axes separately. Treat incident and near-miss files as opportunistic validation or case construction, with exposure and reporting limitations. Silence can remove a hazardous instruction while also omitting needed support, so evaluate absent output and fallback explicitly.

**Datasets and fault design.** Use a real issue-time forecast archive with timestamps and horizons, vessel/route context, and independent reference observations where available. Audit temporal availability and provenance with a sample before locking the study. Existing replay and ERA5 can generate episodes and check climatological representativeness. Reanalysis on its own cannot represent the information available to an operator at decision time. Request an independent coast or forecast regime with different vessel/season mix for a transportability test. Where coverage is limited, report a held-out season and source comparison as within-domain robustness. Synthetic scenarios should probe a registered factorial of latency/staleness, single-channel and correlated block dropout, bias/noise around thresholds and conflicting inputs. Tie magnitudes to observed error or logistics, retain the same underlying hazard reference across perturbations, and distinguish corrupted inputs from a genuinely shifted outcome process. Avoid corrupting label evidence in lockstep with the system input. [ERA5 data documentation](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/pages/viewpage.action?navigatingVersions=true&pageId=242075393), [Copernicus Marine archive guidance](https://help.marine.copernicus.eu/en/articles/4922042-does-copernicus-marine-provide-access-to-product-archives), [predictive uncertainty under shift](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/8558cb408c1d76621371888657d2eb1d-Paper.pdf)

**Metrics.** Use at least one severe inappropriate recommendation per eligible episode as a primary exposure measure. Separately count missed critical cues and report a severity-weighted loss sensitivity analysis. Measure useful coverage as the fraction of eligible episodes with at least one acceptable, actionable, feasible next step, with strict scoring of generic checklists or no advice. Report harmful-output rate among answered episodes, both kinds of denominators, coverage by advice type, false suppression, underrestriction, fallback availability, and risk–useful-coverage curves. Fix how mixed messages containing both a useful step and a dangerous instruction count. Treat compliance, structural binding, and changes in displayed advice as diagnostics. A separate counterbalanced randomized vignette study can estimate reliance and choice. Real-world injury effects need their own outcome design.

**Analysis.** Pair policies on the same episode and candidate message. Split development, calibration and testing by site or time blocks and avoid shared weather events across splits. Stratify on preintervention strata and report actual paired discordance. Use storm/day/voyage or suitable hierarchical clustering and blocked or cluster bootstrap intervals. An exact McNemar test is applicable to independent paired binary episodes. For clustered or repeated fault cases use cluster-level resampling or an appropriately specified mixed model, with rater dependence carried through where labels are repeated. Define the main contrast, practical margin and multiplicity strategy before testing. Estimate fault interactions with uncertainty bands and show out-of-site replication separately. Enrich rare binding and near-boundary cases for mechanism discovery, then recover overall prevalence via a representative cohort or known sampling weights. Plan precision using pilot discordance and independent episode clusters, alongside conservative assumptions about severe-event frequency. Never count every hour or synthetic variant as an independent observation. Report inconclusive results and the prevalence of indeterminate reference judgments.

**Scientific requirements.** A deterministic, executable offline evaluator or equivalent controlled experimental harness, populated advisory candidates, a frozen common comparison design, independent labels, held-out testing and a reproducible analysis are necessary. Robustness claims require realistic issue-time or independently characterized perturbations. A light rules or classical model can supply the candidate messages. A second generator is useful for checking mechanism dependence. An LLM is unnecessary unless the claim concerns generative advice, and then the study would need stable model versions, replicated seeds and semantic evaluation. A deployed app, autonomous operation, prospective field exposure and hardware measurements are unnecessary for this recommendation-level claim. Report computation, memory and latency on representative hardware only if the paper claims a deployment envelope. Measure decision-time availability of information and fallback even in offline experiments, since those are central to the low-resource setting.

## 9. Expected contribution to knowledge

If supported, the study could estimate the paired incremental benefit of category-level filtering, identify the useful coverage it preserves at comparable harm exposure, and describe where that benefit disappears under measured information failures. For example, the study could show that timely but incomplete observations still justify a verification recommendation, while stale or discrepant observations undermine the very distinction on which filtering depends. This would be a conditional empirical result about the tested outputs, populations and information regimes. Replication across coastlines or generators can support a bounded transportability claim.

A precise null, crossover or dominance by tuned binary abstention would also address the scientific question if labels and comparator calibration are credible. Existing work already supplies the gating mechanism and evidence that it sometimes changes admissibility. This proposed study targets recommendation-level appropriateness and actionability. Consequences for actual departures or accident rates would require distinct evidence.

## 11. Research risks

| Threat | Consequence | Control or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Originality and overlap | A third architecture comparison duplicates formal/fidelity/replay contributions or repeats prior shielding work. | Reuse the architecture as a fixed instrument, document relation to the conference drafts and Journal 1, and make the independent endpoint and comparator the test. |
| Construct validity | Experts ratify the same rules or classify a rule-compliant generic statement as safe useful advice. | Independently justify safety, check reference provenance against the tested rules, and score actionability, omission and available fallback separately. |
| Internal validity | Shared effects of the generator, interface wording or threshold tuning are attributed to the intermediate filter. | Freeze candidates, align status cues, blind labels, and compare with shared and separately tuned cutoffs for different estimands. |
| Statistical conclusion validity | Sparse severe discordance, correlated weather hours and many perturbations inflate apparent precision. | Use event-cluster precision planning, prespecified margins and endpoints, and enough independent episodes to identify effect or uncertainty. |
| External validity | One coast, vessel class or model makes the result dependent on a particular threshold arrangement. | Test external regime or generator replication, characterize target populations and report bounded claims where feasible data are limited. |
| Reproducibility | Forecast archives, labels and candidate outputs are inaccessible or change. | Freeze issue-time manifests, hashes, policy versions, rating guide, split IDs and analysis. Share an anonymized or synthetic surrogate alongside code if restrictions require it. |
| Practical feasibility | Required observations, vessel information, local raters or safe replacement steps are unavailable. | Start with an audit of matched records and a small blinded reference exercise. Scope any participant work through the institution's ethics process before recruitment. |

## 12. Final recommendation

**Revise before committing to implementation.** Proceed to a protocol and data-feasibility stage with four measurable exit criteria.

1. Confirm one well-defined predeparture decision and obtain issue-time data with independent hazard evidence for enough materially different vessel/weather episodes.
2. Demonstrate that blinded raters can consistently distinguish plausibly hazardous specific advice, useful lower-specificity guidance, omissions and justified silence. Examine disagreements with a maritime specialist.
3. Establish candidate diversity, enough changed delivered messages and enough independent discordant episodes to estimate an operationally material effect. Check that a binary comparator has access to the same evidence and realistic fallback options.
4. Update citation tracing and licensed searches around the specific independent-label comparison and document overlap with the existing Journal 1 evidence plan.

Advance if those criteria are met. If safety labels can only be derived from the gate, or the available cases offer too little precision to distinguish a meaningful gain from zero, the proposed safety-effectiveness claim is a **no-go**. A strong negative result is publishable in principle when the experiment can resolve the claimed advantage within useful margins.
