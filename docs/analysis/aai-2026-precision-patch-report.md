## C. Why each change was required

1. **Comparator design demoted.** Miller 2026 §3.4 performs *"a systematic ablation study"* across three governance configurations including an ungoverned baseline, with Mann–Whitney U and Cliff's δ. Presenting generic comparator methodology as a contribution would be refutable by a single citation. The ladder is retained in the method — it performs the specific isolation — but its *status* changed, not its presence.
2. **Sensitivity split.** Kim et al. 2026 sweep α from 50–100% and report Gray Zone proportion and ΓArea across it, on real FDA-cleared algorithm data. The technique is established; the sensitivity *of a contraction-binding rate* is not, within the reviewed set.
3. **Denominator and lower bound reclassified.** Neither is a finding. Denominator dependence shows the reported rate varies with an analyst choice; the lower bound follows from monotone aggregation. Both are honest disclosures and both remain in the paper — as qualifications.
4. **Component attribution promoted.** It answers what actually produces the intermediate state in the evaluated record (`g_o` 518/531 = 97.6%), which no reviewed source provides.
5. **Attack row added.** The chain had no row covering "nothing beyond prior art" once three near-neighbours exist. The defence recorded is that the measured object and the isolation differ — **not** that the techniques are new.
6. **Cluster D added.** Without it the paper implicitly claims three established techniques.
