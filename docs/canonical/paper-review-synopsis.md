Our architecture is a state-conditioned instantiation of GS principles that fills a gap the framework leaves open — how to govern under conditions that are elevated but not prohibitive. | The closest theoretical precedent; positioning our architecture as a domain-specific instantiation of GS principles is a core framing decision. |

| Flehmig et al. (2024) [[notes]](../../notes/Implementing%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20in%20Safety-Critical%20Systems%20during%20Operation-%20Challenges%20and%20Extended%20Framework%20for%20a%20Quality%20Assurance%20Process.md) | Implements a traffic-light degradation index for AI in safety-critical systems, classifying AI performance into three levels (green / orange / red) using non-AI statistical methods; at orange, the supervisory component conducts thorough checks; at red, the system switches to a non-AI backup. | The closest structural precedent — three-level classification triggered by monitoring, with an intermediate level. (2026) — AgentSpec [[notes]](../../notes/AgentSpec-%20Customizable%20Runtime%20Enforcement%20for%20Safe%20and%20Reliable%20LLM%20Agents.md) | Domain-specific language for runtime enforcement over LLM agents, allowing developers to define structured rules composed of triggers, predicates, and enforcement actions; prevents over 90% of unsafe code executions in the reported evaluation. | State-of-the-art in per-action runtime enforcement. ## Reading rules

- **Ordering.** Both tables are alphabetical by first author, matching Table 2 of `review-protocol.md` and Table 3a of `master-coding-table.md`.
- **Notes link.** Each row's `[[notes]]` link opens the paper's full extraction notes. Follow it for the paper's raw contribution details, methodology, per-dimension governance analysis, and citation trail.
- **Framing convention.** *What the paper does* is written in our framing so that the description maps directly onto our comparison scheme. Papers themselves may use different vocabulary; we prefer to translate consistently.
- **What "Our comment" covers.** Interpretation, not description. What is right about the approach, what is missing relative to our architecture, and — where relevant — why the missing part is load-bearing for our contribution. Comments should be defensible against the paper's own claims; the extraction notes carry the evidence.
- **What "Why we selected it" covers.** The argumentative role the paper plays in the review, in one sentence. Not the paper's importance in general; the paper's importance *to our argument*.
