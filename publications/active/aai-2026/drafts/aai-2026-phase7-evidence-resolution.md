**Recovered thresholds.** The wave block evaluates **six (O_C, O_U) pairs**, not a sweep. Values in §3.

### Why the recovered data cannot produce the locked figure

**(a) There is no wave-onset sweep.** Only three of the six configurations vary the CAUTION onset: O_C ∈ {0.75, 1.00, 1.25}. Three points do not make a trace, and nothing in the repository evaluates intermediate onsets.

**(b) The third point is confounded.** The configuration labelled *"sens +0.25 on O_C"* is **(1.25, 1.50)** — it moves the UNSAFE boundary from 1.25 m to 1.50 m at the same time. It is not a pure O_C variation.

**(c) Δ<sub>L2</sub> is not single-valued in the wave CAUTION onset.** Two canonical configurations share O_C = 1.00 and return different values — 5.81% at O_U = 1.25 and 9.22% at O_U = 1.90. A plot of Δ<sub>L2</sub> against the onset would place two different *y* values at the same *x*.

**(d) The sourced-threshold region is not a region on this axis.** The 5.67–9.56% envelope is generated by varying the rainfall CAUTION boundary R_C across five settings and the wave UNSAFE boundary O_U across two, **all at O_C = 1.00**. It is a spread at a single *x* position, not a band spanning the wave-onset axis. Drawing it as a shaded band across that axis would assert a relationship the experiment never evaluated.

**Baseline checks: PASS.** Both baselines reproduce, and the script's own identity check reports `IDENTITY HOLDS` for each configuration.

**Perturbation check: PASS on the range, FAIL on the stated gradient.** See §9, Action 1.

**Caption compatibility: CAPTION REVIEW REQUIRED.** The locked caption promises a trace per configuration, a shaded sourced-threshold band and a marked operational-ceiling reading. Points (a)–(d) mean none of the first three can be drawn honestly from canonical data.

**Figure NOT generated.** No `aai-2026-figure1.png` or `.pdf` was produced. Generating one would have required interpolating onsets, silently dropping the confounded point or re-plotting the envelope as an axis band — all forbidden by A3.

### FINDING 3 — FOR DECISION, NOT ACTION (withheld RESOLUTION components)
